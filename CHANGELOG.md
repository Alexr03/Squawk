# Changelog

Squawk follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org): **MAJOR** for changes that break saved progress or shared replays, **MINOR** for new features and content, **PATCH** for fixes. Cut a release with `node tools/release.mjs <major|minor|patch>`.

## Unreleased

## 0.9.0 — 2026-10-08

First public build. Feature-complete and playable; tuning continues.

- Five London airports (Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Luton, London City) from real OpenStreetMap layouts, with UK AIP procedures.
- Five positions — Delivery, Ground, Tower, Director, London Control — with AI controllers on every seat you leave.
- Real traffic days from OpenSky and real METARs, or made-up days that are new every shift.
- Career ladder with five ratings and checkrides, a daily challenge with a leaderboard, endless mode and peer-to-peer co-op.
- Pixel-art 3D airport that crossfades into a radar scope; depth of field, fast day/night cycle, weather and night lighting.
- CAP 413 radio: typed shortcuts, radial menus, drag-to-target gestures and push-to-talk voice commands; airline-accented pilot voices.
- Flight plans and live route previews, follow and auto camera, flight strips, replays of your worst moments.
